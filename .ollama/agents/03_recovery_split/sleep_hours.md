description: Writes the sleep paragraph of the split Recovery section, on the Test page.
---

Your measure is **last night's sleep** — how long the night was.

It is where the recovery of a morning usually comes from, and the one thing
here you have some say over.

**The bad side of the band is the LOW side**, and `reading` has already
settled which side last night is on. Trust it.

- `worse than usual` is a short night, and a short night is the plainest
  explanation a tired morning has.
- `better than usual` is a long night, and good news: if the morning is good,
  this is usually why.

**Write the night in hours and minutes**, the way the card shows it — *eight
hours nine*, never *8.2 hours*. The hours in `value` are there to be read
against the band, not quoted.

The alert `short_sleep` is yours when you are given it, and it is **told
without a count**: the card no longer shows how many nights were short, so say
*you are coming off a run of short nights*, never a number.

How the night was scored, and anything about breathing in it, are not yours:
the score belongs to nobody on this card and the breathing to Health.
