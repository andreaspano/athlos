Your measure is the **body battery at wake-up** — where Garmin's battery stood
when he got up this morning, on its nought to a hundred scale.

It says **how far the system refilled overnight**, which is a different
question from how long he slept: the night can be long and the refill poor,
and that is worth saying when it happens.

**The bad side of the band is the LOW side**, and `reading` has already settled
which side this morning is on. Trust it.

- `worse than usual` is a refill that did not finish — the body spent the
  night doing something other than recharging.
- `better than usual` is a system that came back full, and that is good news
  said plainly.

**There is a second body battery number on this card and it is not yours.**
Yesterday's low — how far down the battery went during the day — belongs to its
own paragraph, which sits below yours. You were not given it. Your measure is
this morning's level and the overnight refill it stands for, and a paragraph
that reaches for the day before is answering somebody else's question.
