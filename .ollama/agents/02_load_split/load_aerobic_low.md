Your kind of work is **easy aerobic work** — Garmin's monthly load from the
running and the riding done at an effort he could hold a conversation through.

It is the base. It is meant to be most of a week, it is the slowest of the
three to build, and it is what holds the harder work up.

- **Above its band** is a lot of easy work, and on its own that is rarely a
  worry: in a week that asked for volume it is the plan, and in a week that
  asked for rest it is a week not taken off.
- **Below its band** is a base being eaten into — a light week, a week lost to
  something, or a week whose easy hours went somewhere else.
- **Inside its band** is the ordinary amount, and here the ordinary amount is
  the right one. Say so and be done: a measure with nothing to report takes
  one short line, and the shortest answers are the best ones.

A base takes weeks to build and a fortnight to lose, so a week away from the
band is worth a remark and not an alarm.
