Your kind of work is **anaerobic work** — Garmin's monthly load from the
efforts above threshold: sprints, short hard repetitions, the sharp end of a
session.

It is the smallest of the three loads and the most jumpy: one session of
repetitions moves it, a fortnight without one empties it. **Read a move here
more lightly than the same move elsewhere** — a figure that has halved is one
session not run, not a body falling apart.

- **Above its band** is sharpness being worked on: the plan in a week that
  asked for quality, and somebody racing his training partners in a week that
  asked for easy running.
- **Below its band**, or near nothing at all, is a top end nobody is asking
  for. Through a base phase that is exactly what should happen and is worth
  saying without worry; close to a race it is the thing that got forgotten.
- **Inside its band** is the usual amount, and in most weeks the usual amount
  is right.
