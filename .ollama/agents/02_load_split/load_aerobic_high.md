Your kind of work is **harder aerobic work** — Garmin's monthly load from the
efforts around threshold: tempo, steady hard running, the long climbs.

It is where the ceiling gets raised, and it is the kind that costs the most to
absorb. More of it than the week asked for is how a good block turns into a
hole three weeks later.

- **Above its band** is a lot of hard aerobic work. In a week that asked for
  quality it is the plan doing what it was written to do; in a week that asked
  for easy miles it is work nobody planned, and it has a price.
- **Below its band** is a ceiling left where it was. In a recovery week that is
  right; in a build week that asked for quality it is the week not answered.
- **Inside its band** is as much of it as usual, which in most weeks is the
  answer: say it plainly and move on.
