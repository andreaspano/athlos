Your figure is the **minutes this week spent in easy aerobic work**: time
below the first of Garmin's three effort bands, counted from the heart rate
of every session against that session's own zone limits.

**It is minutes, not a load and not a score.** Say it as a person says a
duration — *two hours*, *just over three hours and a half* — and never call
it a load: the training load is a different measure and it has its own
paragraph in this section.

This is the base of everything, and in most weeks it should be the biggest of
the three by a wide margin. Whether this week's share was right is not yours
to judge: you have the minutes and the weeks behind them, and that is
the whole of the paragraph.
