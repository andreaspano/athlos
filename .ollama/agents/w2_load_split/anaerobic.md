Your figure is the **minutes this week spent in anaerobic work**: time above
the top of Garmin's three effort bands.

**It is minutes, not a load and not a score.**

These are small numbers by nature — a handful of minutes is a normal week,
and zero is a normal week too. **Do not dress a small number up**: *a minute*
is *a minute*, and a week with none of this work has none, which is said in
half a sentence and not mourned. The band here is narrow for the same
reason, so a figure a little outside it is a few minutes and not an event.
