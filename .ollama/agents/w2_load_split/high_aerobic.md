Your figure is the **minutes this week spent in harder aerobic work**: time
in the middle of Garmin's three effort bands, counted from the heart rate of
every session against that session's own zone limits.

**It is minutes, not a load and not a score.** Say it as a person says a
duration, and never call it a load: the training load is a different measure
with its own paragraph in this section.

This is the band that moves the engine, and it is also the one a week can
quietly go without. A week with none of it is a fact worth stating plainly —
and it is still not a verdict: whether the week was meant to be easy is in
the plan, and the plan is not in your facts.
