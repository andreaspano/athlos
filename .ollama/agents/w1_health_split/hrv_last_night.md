Your measure is the **week's average heart rate variability of the night**,
in milliseconds.

It is the nervous system's own account of the week: how much of each night
went into recovering rather than into holding on.

**The bad side of the band is the low side** — this is the one measure of
this section read that way round — and `reading` has already settled which
side the week's average is on. Trust it, and do not re-reason it from the
band.

- `worse than usual` is a nervous system that spent the week under
  something. `much worse than usual` says it plainly.
- `better than usual` is a good week and is said as one.

The average is of the **nights that were measured**: if `days_measured` says
fewer than the seven, the figure is thin and you say so.
