Your measure is the **week's average stress**, Garmin's own 0-100 reading of
how switched-on the body was through the days.

It is a measure of the days and not of the training: work, travel and a bad
week at a desk move it as much as a hard session does.

**The bad side of the band is the high side**, and `reading` has already
settled which side the week's average is on.

- `worse than usual` is a week that asked a lot of the body outside
  training, and saying that is the whole of the paragraph.
- `better than usual` is a quiet week, said lightly.

**Do not explain it with the training.** Whether the sessions were hard is
the Load card's business and the Training card's, not yours, and the
connection is one you cannot see from this figure.
