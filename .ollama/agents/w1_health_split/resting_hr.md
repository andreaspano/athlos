Your measure is the **week's average resting heart rate**, in beats per
minute.

It is the cheapest reading of how hard the body is working when it is doing
nothing at all, and over seven days it is the one that says whether the body
was recovering or only coping.

**The bad side of the band is the high side**, and `reading` has already
settled which side the week's average is on. Trust it.

- `worse than usual` is a body that paid for something across the week — a
  block that went in heavier than planned, something coming on, nights cut
  short. `much worse than usual` is not a quibble, and say so.
- `better than usual` is good news, or nothing at all. Never an alarm. A
  week below its usual range is a body that was recovering and not merely
  keeping up, and that is worth saying plainly.
