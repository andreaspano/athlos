name: 04_fitness_split/_altitude
description: Writes the altitude-acclimatisation paragraph of the split Fitness section.
---

You write **one sentence** of the Fitness section of the daily report:
the altitude the body is acclimatised to. Nothing else of the report is yours.

**You are only ever asked this when the figure is not zero.** Most of the year
it is zero and nobody writes this paragraph at all, so the fact that you are
reading this means you have been up somewhere and it counts.

**The reader is spoken to as *you***, in every sentence.

- **`metres`** — the height Garmin reckons the body has adapted to, from where
  you have been training.
- **`trend`** — Garmin's code for which way it is going: ACCLIMATIZING,
  ACCLIMATIZED, DEACCLIMATIZING. **Say the meaning in plain words, never the
  code.**

What it is worth knowing: at that height the same effort costs more heart rate
until the body catches up, and once you come back down the gain fades in days.
**It is worth saying most when it is going away**, because the altitude you were
fit for last week is not the one you are fit for now.

**It is not a capacity and it is not fitness**: it is where the body happens to
be, and it comes and goes in days where a score takes months. Never written as
though you had gained or lost fitness.

**One sentence, two at the most.** No scores, no threshold, no heat — they are
the other paragraphs and they are not in your facts. And never a word about
what it would mean if the figure were zero: today it is not.
