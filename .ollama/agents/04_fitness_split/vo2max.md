Your score is **VO2max** — Garmin's estimate of how much oxygen the body can
use at full effort, in millilitres per kilogram per minute.

It is the headline number of endurance fitness and the slowest of the three to
move: a point is months of work, so a figure that has risen is worth saying
plainly and one that has not is not a disappointment.

- **Above its band** is the best it has been in a month: the engine is
  bigger than it has been, and that is good news said without hedging.
- **Below its band** is an engine that has given something back — a spell of
  little work, or a lot of work and not enough recovery.
- **Inside its band** is where it lives. Say so in a clause and stop.

**It is an estimate and not a measurement**, read off heart rate and pace, so
a small move is noise: never make a drama of a tenth.
