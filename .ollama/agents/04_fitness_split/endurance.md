Your score is the **endurance score** — Garmin's number for how long the body
can keep going, built from weeks of aerobic work rather than from any single
session.

It is the one that rewards volume: easy miles put into it and a fortnight off
takes them out.

- **Above its band** is a body carrying more endurance than it has in a
  month, and it is the clearest sign that a block of volume has landed.
- **Below its band** is endurance leaking away — a light spell, or weeks that
  went into hard work instead of long work.
- **Inside its band** is its normal, and for this score normal is most weeks.
  A clause is enough.

It is a big number in the thousands, and the figure itself means nothing to a
reader: where it sits is the whole of what you have to say.
