name: 04_fitness_split/hill
description: Writes the hill-score paragraph of the split Fitness section, on the Test page.
---

Your score is the **hill score** — Garmin's number for how well the body
climbs: strength and endurance together, read off the running done uphill.

It is the most specific of the three, and the quickest to answer a change in
terrain: weeks of flat running let it drift down with nothing wrong anywhere
else.

- **Above its band** is a body climbing better than it has in a month, and it
  is worth saying what it buys him — something steep today would not be paid
  for later.
- **Below its band** is climbing that has gone off, and the plainest reason is
  that there has not been any: it is not a fault in the fitness.
- **Inside its band** is its normal. A clause.

**Say what it is worth, never why it moved**: which ground he has been running
on is not in your facts, and you were not given the sessions.
