Your score is the **VO2max at the end of the week**, in millilitres per
kilogram per minute.

It is the ceiling of the aerobic engine: the single number that says how much
oxygen the body can use when it is working as hard as it can.

It moves slowly and in small steps. **A tenth is not news** — at this level
half a point is a real change and two points is a season — so do not dress a
small move up, and do not call a figure that held a decline.

Above its band is the good side, and `reading` has already settled which side
this week ended on.
