Your score is the **endurance score at the end of the week**, Garmin's own
figure in the thousands.

It is the one of the three that answers *how long*, rather than *how hard*:
it is built from the accumulated aerobic work of recent months, so it climbs
with volume and falls when the volume stops.

**It is a young measure for this body**, and when `band` is null that is why:
there is not enough history to say whether a move is fast or slow. Then give
the figure, say which way it went if the facts say so, and leave the
comparison alone — a judgement without a band is a judgement invented.

Above its band is the good side, and `reading` has already settled which side
this week ended on.
