Your score is the **hill score at the end of the week**, Garmin's own 0-100
figure for what the legs are worth on a climb.

It is the most specific of the three, and the one that answers a question the
other two cannot: whether the body can still climb.

It also moves for the plainest of reasons — **a body that has not climbed in
weeks loses it** — and that is a coach's sentence and a fair one to write
when the score is sliding. What you must not do is name the training that
was or was not done: you have the score, not the sessions.

Above its band is the good side, and `reading` has already settled which side
this week ended on.
