Your measure is the **average stress of yesterday**, on Garmin's nought to a
hundred scale, read from the whole day and not from the night.

It is the only one of the four that is about the day just gone rather than
the night just past, and it is as much about work and life as about training.

**The bad side of the band is the high side**, and `reading` has already
settled which side yesterday sits on. Trust it.

- `worse than usual` is a day the body spent switched on.
- `better than usual` is a quiet day, and that is good news.

No alert is yours.
