Your measure is the **resting heart rate of this morning**, in beats per
minute.

It is the cheapest reading of how hard the body is working when it is doing
nothing at all.

**The bad side of the band is the high side**, and `reading` has already
settled which side this morning's value is on. Trust it.

- `worse than usual` is a body paying for something — a night cut short,
  something coming on, a hard day not yet absorbed. `much worse than usual`
  is not a quibble, and say so.
- `better than usual` is good news, or nothing at all. Never an alarm.

The alert `resting_hr_up` is yours when you are given it.
