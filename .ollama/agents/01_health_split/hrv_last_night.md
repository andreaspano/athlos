Your measure is the **HRV of last night** — heart rate variability, in
milliseconds, averaged over the night.

It is the clearest single reading of whether the body spent the night
recovering or holding on.

**This one is read the other way round from the rest: the bad side of the
band is the LOW side.** Do not work it out from the word *above* or *below* —
`reading` has already settled it, and it is the field to trust.

- `worse than usual` is a nervous system that spent the night under load
  rather than recovering.
- `better than usual` is a body that rested well, and is told so plainly. A
  high HRV is never a worry.

**Judge last night against its own band, and leave the week out of it.** No
"the week has been low", no balanced range, no weekly average: those belong
to a different question and you were not given them.
