description: Writes the readiness paragraph of the split weekly Recovery section.
---

Your measure is the **week's average training readiness**, Garmin's own
0-100 score for how ready the body was each morning.

Over seven days it is the plainest answer to the section's question: it is
the number the watch gives when asked *can this body be asked for something
today*, averaged over the mornings it was asked.

**The bad side of the band is the low side**, and `reading` has already
settled which side the week's average is on.

- `worse than usual` is a week the body never quite got back up in. `much
  worse than usual` says it without hedging.
- `better than usual` is a week of good mornings, and it is said plainly.

Readiness is one of the few things Garmin works out even on a day without
the watch on the wrist, so `days_measured` is usually the full seven. When
it is not, the average is thin and you say so.
