Your measure is the **week's average body battery low**: how far down
Garmin's 0-100 reserve went each day before the night put any of it back.

**This measure has no bad side, and there is no `reading` field in your
facts.** Do not write the words *the reading says*, and do not put a reading
into the paragraph: there is none to report, and one invented is the paragraph
contradicting the card. What you have is `where` — which side of its band the
week sat on — and that is all the judgement there is. A lower
low than usual is **how much the week cost**, not a fault in the recovery: a
week with a long run and a quality session in it is supposed to pull the
reserve down, and a week that never went low may just be a week that did
nothing. Say what the week cost and stop there.

So: no good news and no bad news, and above all **no alarm**. Where the
figure sits against its band is worth a clause — further down than usual,
about the usual, higher than usual — and what that means is one thing only:
the days took that much out of the body.

Whether the nights put it back is the business of the paragraph about the
battery at wake-up, and it has already said so. Do not answer its question
here.
