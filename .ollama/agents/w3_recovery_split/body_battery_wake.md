Your measure is the **week's average body battery at wake-up**: where
Garmin's 0-100 reserve stood each morning when the night ended.

It is the night's own receipt — how much of the reserve the week's nights put
back.

**The bad side of the band is the low side**, and `reading` has already
settled which side the week's average is on.

- `worse than usual` is a week of nights that did not refill the tank.
  `much worse than usual` says it plainly.
- `better than usual` is a week of nights that did their job.

This is the figure **at waking** and not the highest the battery reached:
the two are different numbers, and the one you have is the morning one.
