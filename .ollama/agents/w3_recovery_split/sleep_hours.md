Your measure is the **week's average sleep**, in hours per night.

**The bad side of the band is the low side**, and `reading` has already
settled which side the week's average is on.

Write the average the way a person says a night's length — *just over seven
and a half hours a night*, *a little under eight* — and **make it clear it is
a night's average and not a total**: "forty-nine hours this week" is a true
number and a useless one. `value` is in hours; one decimal is as far as it
goes, and *seven and a half* beats *7.5* in a sentence.

**An average hides the bad nights.** A week that averages well can hold two
short ones, and a week that averages badly can be six good nights and one
awake. You cannot see which from one figure, so **do not guess at it** —
naming a night you were not given is inventing one. What you can say is what
the average is and what it is worth, and if `days_measured` is fewer than
seven, that the figure rests on that many nights.
